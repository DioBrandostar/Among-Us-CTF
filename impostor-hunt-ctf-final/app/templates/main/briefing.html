FILE #2147-ORION: MISSION BRIEFING

Interstellar Recovery Authority — Classified

In the year 2147, the deep-space industrial ship Orion-9 was returning to Earth after a mining operation near Jupiter's outer colonies. The ship carried engineers, security officers, technicians, scientists, and navigation crew. What was supposed to be a routine return trip became a silent disaster.

A few days before reaching Earth, communication with the ship suddenly stopped. No distress signal. No emergency meeting. No escape pods launched. Only silence.

When recovery teams finally intercepted the drifting vessel, they found most of the crew dead, systems partially sabotaged, and the ship locked under emergency containment protocols.

The worst part: The main Emergency Button system was still functional. But nobody used it.

You have been sent as a cyber-security specialist to investigate the incident aboard Orion-9. At first, the ship appears abandoned. Lights flicker. Doors malfunction. Crew terminals remain logged in. Audio logs replay fragments of panic.

The truth is that one of the crew members was an impostor — a human infiltrator planted to steal classified mining data. They manipulated systems, sabotaged communications, and turned the ship into a trap.

THE AUTHORIZATION LOCK

When suspicion started spreading, the captain attempted to initiate an Emergency Meeting to expose the impostor. But the Emergency Button no longer accepted command authorization.

The impostor had secretly changed the administrator credentials. Without valid admin access, the system was useless, and the crew was defenseless.

YOUR MISSION

Recover the stolen administrator credentials and reactivate the Emergency System before the ship reaches Earth's orbit. The impostor may still be alive somewhere aboard the vessel.
